Spice Sort — Support

Spice Sort is a calm, fully offline sorting puzzle for iPhone. Pour ground spices between glass jars until every jar holds a single color. No ads, no accounts, no purchases.

Frequently asked

I'm stuck on a level. Every level is guaranteed solvable. Undo is unlimited, and you can restart the level any time with the circular-arrow button.

Did I lose my progress? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes progress; reinstalling starts fresh.

Colors are hard to tell apart. Turn on Colorblind Assist in Settings — every spice gets a distinct pattern.

Does it work offline? Yes, completely. Airplane mode is fine.
