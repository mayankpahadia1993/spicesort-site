Spice Sort — Privacy Policy

Spice Sort is a fully offline puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and collects no personal information.

What the app collects

The app may send a small set of anonymous usage signals via TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These signals contain no personal identifiers — only a one-way hashed anonymous identifier generated on your device, which cannot be linked to you. The current App Store version ships with these analytics disabled, so the app collects nothing at all.

SignalData included
App launched / session endedSession length (bucketed)
Level started / completed / abandonedLevel number, move count, duration, undo count
Daily puzzle completedStreak length
Setting changedWhich setting (e.g. sound, colorblind mode)

What the app does not collect

No location. No device identifiers usable for tracking. No contacts. No names, emails, or any personally identifiable information. No cross-app or cross-site data. Nothing is ever sold or shared with advertisers.

Offline play

The game is fully playable with no network connection. Analytics signals are the app's only network traffic and are simply skipped when you're offline.

Your progress

Game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/mayankpahadia1993/spicesort-site.
